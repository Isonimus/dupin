The Dupin Manifesto

Dupin is a defensive intelligence toolkit for Wi-Fi environments.

Dupin does not attack.
Dupin does not impersonate.
Dupin does not interfere.

Dupin observes.

Where offensive tools seek impact, Dupin seeks understanding.
Where attacks exploit trust, Dupin studies it.
Where noise overwhelms, Dupin looks for patterns.

Dupin assumes that:

Normality is local

Anomalies precede incidents

Context matters more than signatures

Dupin works at the edge, close to the signal, close to the environment.
It builds memory, not targets.
It forms hypotheses, not verdicts.

Dupin exists to answer one question:

“What changed — and why does it matter?”
